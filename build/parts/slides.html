THE TWO PATHS

Same ₱5,000, Two Starting Points

One starts from what we have. The other starts from what we want.
EFFECTUAL Start from the means "We have ₱5,000, an oven and five of us. What can we make?"
We decide by what we can afford to lose — so the goal stays open.
CAUSAL Start from the goal "We want ₱6,000 a month by December. What do we need to get there?"
We decide by the plan that hits the number — so the goal comes first.
Terms as defined by Saras D. Sarasvathy, "Causation and Effectuation," Academy of Management Review, 2001.
EFFECTUAL · WHAT WE COULD START

Three Things We Can Make at Home

No stall, no permit, no equipment we do not already own.
01 Coffee & cookies Bottled iced coffee with a pack of homemade cookies.
Start-up ₱3,500 Earns ₱35 a bottle, ₱20 a pack
02 Cookies only Cheapest to start — we already have the oven.
Start-up ₱1,500 Earns ₱20 a pack
03 Iced tea only Cheaper to make than coffee, but it sells for less.
Start-up ₱2,500 Earns ₱18 a bottle
We pick 01. It earns the most per item, and cookies do not melt on the way to school.
EFFECTUAL · AFFORDABLE LOSS

The Most We Are Willing to Lose

₱700 each from the five of us. No loans, no one else's money.
AT RISK ₱3,500 The insulated bag, the bottles, and the first week of ingredients.
OUT OF THE ₱5,000
Spent ₱3,500
Kept ₱1,500
The ₱1,500 we keep is what lets us restock week two without asking anyone for money.
THE STOP RULE Three weeks. If we have not made back half of the ₱3,500, we stop and sell the bag.
Losing ₱700 each costs us one week of allowance. That is why we can start on Monday instead of arguing about it for a month.
EFFECTUAL · FIRST ACTION

This Week We Spend ₱0 Until Friday

Five days, five jobs. We buy only after the orders are counted.
MONDAY
Day 1
Post the offer in our five class group chats One photo, two prices: ₱60 a bottle, ₱35 a cookie pack. We ask for names, not likes — a name means "I am paying on Monday."
TUESDAY
Day 2
Bake one test batch and taste it together All five of us try it, then we lock the recipe and the pack size. Nothing gets sold that the group has not tasted.
WEDNESDAY
Day 3
Ask permission before we sell anything One of us asks our adviser what selling inside the building needs. If the answer is no, we hand over at the gate instead.
THURSDAY
Day 4
Count the names — 20 orders, or we re-price Under 20 we buy nothing. We drop the price, or we sell cookies only, which costs ₱1,500 instead of ₱3,500.
FRIDAY
Day 5
Buy the ₱3,500 kit and split the jobs Two bake Sunday night, one bottles and chills, two hand over in the morning. We sell for the first time the Monday after.
CAUSAL · THE GOAL, WORKED BACKWARD

₱6,000 a Month Is One Bag a Day

We fix the number first, then keep dividing until it fits in a backpack.
THE GOAL ₱6,000 a month, by December
÷ 4 WEEKS ₱1,500 a week
÷ 3 SELLING DAYS ₱500 a day
WHICH IS 10 + 8 bottles and cookie packs a day
CAN WE CARRY IT? Ten bottles and eight cookie packs is one insulated bag — light enough for one person to bring in.
CAN WE SELL IT? Eighteen items in one 30-minute window, all paid for the night before. Nobody queues, nothing is left over.
CAUSAL · THE MARKET

Our Own Building, Before the 7:30

We sell to the people we are already standing next to.
WHO Classmates and blockmates with morning classes in our building — the ones who leave the house without eating.
WHY THEM They already buy coffee somewhere. We are closer, cheaper than a café, and we are there at the one time they need it.
NOT YET The whole campus, walk-in buyers and delivery. Each one needs a cart, a permit, or hours we do not have.
HOW IT WORKS

Orders close at 8 PM. We bake and bottle at night, chill it, and hand it over in one 30-minute window the next morning.

CAUSAL · EXECUTION STEPS

Four Months, One Job Each

Every step is here because the next one needs it.
01
SEPTEMBER · Launch small and prove the window Mon, Wed, Fri only. Ten bottles and eight cookie packs a day, all pre-paid. One handover spot, one 30-minute window.
₱500 a day
02
OCTOBER · Fix the product and share the load Lock one coffee recipe and add a second cookie flavour. Rotate who bakes each week so nobody carries it alone.
₱650 a day
03
NOVEMBER · Take orders from one more section Open the order form to the floor above and sell a ₱90 combo. Only grow the list as far as one bag can still cover it.
₱800 a day
04
DECEMBER · Hold through finals, then decide Keep the three days through exam week, split the profit five ways, and choose together whether AM Brew continues in January.
₱6,000 a month
Checked every week: items sold, earnings per item, how many pre-orders we actually filled, and cash on hand — which never goes below ₱1,500.
RECAP

We Used Both — in This Order

Effectual to get started. Causal to know where it is going.
THE QUESTION EFFECTUAL SAID CAUSAL SAID
What do we sell? Coffee and cookies, because we can already make both at home. Coffee and cookies, because they earn the most per item we carry.
How do we start? One post, 20 orders, ₱0 spent until Friday. Nothing. A plan does not tell you what to do on Monday.
When are we done? Never fixed — whatever our buyers turn it into. December, ₱6,000 a month. A number we can be wrong about.
